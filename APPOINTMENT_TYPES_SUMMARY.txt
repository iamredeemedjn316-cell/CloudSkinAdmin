================================================================================
USER WORKFLOWS
================================================================================

ADMIN/RECEPTIONIST BOOKING F2F APPOINTMENT:
  1. Click "New Appointment" button
  2. Toggle to "Face-to-Face"
  3. Search for patient name
  4. Select from dropdown OR enter new name
  5. Fill service, practitioner, date, time
  6. Click "Book Appointment"
  → Recorded as F2F, admin/receptionist booked, existing/new client marked

PATIENT BOOKING ONLINE APPOINTMENT:
  1. Patient clicks "New Appointment"
  2. Selects "Online" type
  3. Enters or searches for name
  4. Fills service, practitioner, date, time
  5. Clicks "Book Appointment"
  → Recorded as Online, patient booked, new client created
